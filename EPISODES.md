# Arabic in AR — Mimi & Areej

Episode 01: Ismi wa Tasharraftu

- Website: https://ummumuaz97.github.io/ismi-AR/
- Direct AR: https://ummumuaz97.github.io/ismi-AR/ar.html
- QR: assets/ismi-qr.svg
- Image tracking target: Mimi and Areej Inspect the Tag.png (full uncropped image)
- Video: video gabung ismi wa tasharaaftu.mp4
- Compiled target: assets/ismi.mind (MindAR 1.2.5)

Use HTTPS, Safari on iOS or Chrome on Android. Allow camera access. Present the target on paper or a second screen. Video starts muted; tap the sound button to enable audio. A normal video fallback is on the home page.

## Add another episode

Preserve the published episode URLs so printed QR codes remain valid. For each new episode, upload its image and MP4, compile that image into a MindAR target (.mind), copy the AR page and change its target and media paths, then add a card on index.html. Generate a separate QR to the new episode URL. Camera and tracking behavior must be checked on a real mobile device before printing a batch.

## Dependencies

A-Frame 1.5.0 and MindAR 1.2.5 are loaded on demand from their published CDNs. Internet access is needed. No backend or build step is required. Pages serves the root of the main branch.
